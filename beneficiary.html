Beneficiary Designation

Digitizing a fully paper-based process into a self-service digital experience — a $5M project that launched November 2024, replacing physical forms with an end-to-end flow for retirement beneficiary designation.

Project Overview & Context
Role Sr. Product Designer — inherited as lead mid-project
Timeframe Q4 2023 – Q4 2024 · Launched Nov 2024
Scale $5M project · Delivered to dev
Status Launched, live (November 2024)
The Challenge

The Challenge

Designating a beneficiary on a retirement account was a paper process — clients filled out and submitted a physical form, disconnected from their digital experience. The domain carried significant legal and compliance weight, with data validation rules and information architecture challenges I had to learn as part of doing the work.

Strategic Directive

Strategic Objective

Design a net-new digital beneficiary designation experience from first principles — no existing digital pattern in the product to build from — that handles the full legal complexity of the domain while being intuitive enough for clients doing this for the first time.

Process

PHASE 02

Enforcing Exact 100% Allocation

  • Beneficiary share percentages must sum to exactly 100% — a hard legal requirement, not a soft validation
  • Designed the allocation step to make this requirement clear and self-correctable as clients enter allocations
  • Prevents clients from submitting designations that would later be rejected or cause compliance issues

Reflection

I'd have pushed for broader UX strategy thinking earlier, and a deeper understanding of beneficiary designation's legal constraints, before getting into screens. Across all three projects here, there's a consistent throughline — moving from strength in execution toward strength in direction: understanding a domain deeply enough to set the strategy, not just design the screens that follow from it.
